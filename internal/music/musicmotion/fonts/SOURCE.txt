The fonts in this folder are the latin subsets (woff2) of the Fontsource packages @fontsource/<name>@5.3.0
(https://fontsource.org), each under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (the <name>.LICENSE.txt file next to it):
Inter, Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono, Playfair Display, Bebas Neue and Fraunces.
They are embedded in the pages of a piece as data: URLs (see fonts.go); nothing is loaded from the network.
