# Design

## Context

Sources (HyperFrames, Apache-2.0, `skills/hyperframes/references/` at the commit the docs will name): `story.md` (the one thing, three storylines, devices table), `types/music.md` (song structure becomes edit structure, pictures for the words, the look, tells), `film.md` "On music" (hits 40–190 ms early, reactions 0–45 ms after, `set` for the hit, a word holds a beat, a treatment goes stale after 6 s), `motion.md` (moves, ease by role, springs, exits, overlap, stagger, titles, holds, sizes, rhythm, defaults that look generated, transitions with a cause), `captions.md` (groups by energy, open/close times, hard kill, fit, placement), `review.md` (describe as a stranger, questions, note format, never score). Ours: `assist/plan.go` (`planSystem` KEY=value lines, `planUser`), `propose_script.go` (`scriptSystem`, `scriptUser` with sections and lines, the fixed rule), `scene_draft.go` (`sceneSystem`), `rubric.go` (three criteria), `review.go` (the one-call per-criterion grading from `fix-scene-checks`), `page.go` (`#verse`, word spans with `sung`/`now`), `songmap` (loudness every 0.25 s, sections, beats).

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** the four prompts carry the craft with numbers; the plan gives the video one world and one device the scenes share; the verse reads like captions made by someone who has made many; the operator has the procedure written. **Non-Goals:** HyperFrames' media, footage, maps, TTS and capture sections; a fourth rubric criterion; new routes; changing the checks.

## Decisions

- **D1. Plan lines.** `WORLD=<one line: where it happens and what it looks like>`, `DEVICE=<one of: type carries the argument | a character does the work | one object across the song | life before and after | a number lands | the result first | a question the song answers>`, `HOOK=<the first two seconds, exactly, one line>`. Parsed like the others; a missing or unknown `DEVICE` falls to `type carries the argument` (what a typographic video is) with the plan's `note` saying so. `CONCEPT` is kept; the design proposal's user message receives world and device; the script proposal and the scene writer receive device and hook (the hook only for the first piece). `PlanResult` and `openapi.yaml` gain the three fields.
- **D2. Energy in the script input.** `scriptUser` adds, per section, `loudness: <mean of the map's loudness over the section, 0–1> energy: <rank 1..n, 1 the loudest>`; the instruction's rules (proposal) in six lines, with the numbers: quiet sections pieces of 8–15 s, loud ones one piece per 2–4 bars (`bars = 4 × 60 / bpm` seconds), a breakdown (the quietest section after a loud one) one held piece; the home part named in the answer as `"home": "<part>"` at the top level and used by every chorus piece, escalating (params or an extra) each return; the last piece's part equals the first's or is named as its mirror; a piece over 8 s carries `animate`, a `with` with a `start`, or a layer item; `transition_seconds` on at least two thirds of the seams (0.3–0.6), hard cuts on the rest. The validator does not enforce these (they are craft, the review judges); the fixed rule implements energy and home part (chorus pieces share the part chosen for the first chorus; piece lengths from the section's energy rank).
- **D3. Writer's craft block.** `sceneSystem` gains a block "Craft (measured on shipped films)" of about twenty lines with the numbers of the proposal, in the order: titles land and stay still; vary entrances (the five forms); holds; exits; overlap and stagger; ease by role; hits (early move, `set` on the hit, reaction after); sizes (hero 60–90 % width, quiet 2.5–4 % height, keyword in the accent); the generated-look list as "the reviewer marks these"; one metaphor from the world and the device of the plan (given in the input), never a waveform or bars as the picture. The rubric's `metaphor` criterion gets one clause: "and it belongs to the video's world and device". `SceneDraftInput` gains `World`, `Device`, `Hook` from the plan kept with the piece (the plan's fields are stored with the piece when the flow makes it; a piece made without a plan has them empty and the block says "choose a world and keep it").
- **D4. Reviewer: describe, then score.** `review.go`'s prompt: first, for each of the four frames, one sentence a stranger would say, naming every object (`frames: ["…", …]` in the JSON answer); then the per-criterion scores and notes; the metaphor score is capped at 1 when a frame's sentence contains, as a whole word (never a substring: "barstool", "shaped" do not count), "shape", "shapes", "bars", "blob", "abstract" or "unclear" and the plan's `world` does not name that object (an empty `world` never exempts) (a rule in Go on the model's own description, so the model's description and its score agree); notes in the form `t=4.2s · where · problem → exact change`. The piece review adds two questions whose answers go to `notes`: "with the words covered, do the frames tell the story?" and "does each frame cause the next?". Thresholds unchanged.
- **D5. Verse groups.** In `layerScript`, a line longer than 6 words is split into groups by the section's energy rank (top third: 2–3 words, middle: 3–5, bottom: 4–6), breaking at punctuation or at a gap of 150 ms or more between words, the keyword always inside a group; one group visible at a time, opening 0.2 s before its first word's start (never before the line's start) and hard-killed 0.12 s after its last word's end or at the next group's open; the group of the keyword carries the accent as today. Lines of 6 words or fewer are shown as now. Deterministic from the map. The scene page does the same for the house verse; a scene that owns the verse is told the grouping rule in the craft block.
- **D6. The skill.** `.claude/skills/music-video/SKILL.md` (frontmatter `name`, `description`), sections: when to use it; prerequisites (the server, the token, `GET /api/music-motion/catalog` features to check: `scene`, `theme`, `review`, `word-timing`, `reference-breakdown`); the procedure with the exact routes and bodies, one step per heading, each with its stop point ("show the person X and wait" / "go on") and what to look at (the mode of the design against the piece's modes, the contrast note, `words_source`, `kept_previous`, the review's frame sentences); the craft rules in short; the evidence to leave (`LEEME.txt` naming every file, the piece ids, nothing deleted); what not to do (never delete the owner's pieces, never run `npx` on the server, never score a film without describing it). A `references/craft.md` next to it holds the full rule list shared with the docs. `scripts/check-docs.sh` verifies the routes the skill names exist in `openapi.yaml`.
- **D7. Credit.** `THIRD_PARTY.md`: "Craft rules distilled from the HyperFrames skills (HeyGen, Apache-2.0, commit …) in `docs/MUSIC_MODE_MOTION.md` § Craft, the prompts of the planner, the script, the scenes and the review, and `.claude/skills/music-video/`". The docs' Craft section says which rules were measured by them and which are ours.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Longer prompts cost time on a local model] → the craft block is ~20 lines; the plan's three lines are short; measured in task 5 with the same model as the floor.
- [The grouped verse changes every existing render] → only lines over 6 words change, and only how they are grouped; the words' times are unchanged; a person who wants the whole line can set `verse_style` as today (a `verse_groups: false` design field is one line, added here).
- [The description cap on the metaphor score punishes an honest "abstract" world] → the cap applies to words that mean the viewer could not tell what it was; a deliberate abstract world is named in `WORLD` and the reviewer is told it.
- [A plan made outside the flow has no world/device] → the writer is told to choose one and keep it; the piece stores the first scene's choice as `world` for the next scenes (a field on the piece, written by the batch).
